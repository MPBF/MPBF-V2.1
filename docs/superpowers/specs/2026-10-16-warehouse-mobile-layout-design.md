# Compact warehouse navigation on phones

Approved by the user: on phones, keep all three authorized warehouse tabs in one
row: صالة الإنتاج / المنتجات التامة / صالة الخام. Rename the finished-stock
section to المنتجات التامة. Hide the hall and finished-stock summary statistics
on phones only, retaining them on tablet and desktop.

Preserve individual stock/order quantities, balances, receiving and raw-material
movement controls, permissions and routes. Scope styling to warehouse screens,
not the manufacturing or planning dashboards. Raw stock has no equivalent
summary-stat strip; preserve its material balances and operational notices.

Verify both languages at 320, 390, 430, 768 and 1440 pixels using isolated browser
fixtures, including navigation, clipping, touch-target height and retained stock
controls. Do not change backend data or authorization.
