---
name: Customer profile product ordering
description: User-approved ordering preference scoped to the customer profile.
---

Products in the customer profile should be arranged alphabetically by category **name**, not category code, with unclassified products at the end.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected alphabetical category-name ordering rather than category-code ordering.

**How to apply:** Keep this preference scoped to the customer profile. Do not infer that other product lists or category administration should use the same ordering.
