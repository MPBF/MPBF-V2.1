name: Approved leave and displayed absence
description: User-approved absence policy, dated leave, and safeguards for undated legacy approvals.
---

The user chose to show “غائب” only for assigned workdays whose attendance
window has ended, excluding approved leave and dates before employment.
Ongoing/overnight shifts must not be marked absent early; recorded attendance
always takes precedence over derived absence or leave.

**Why:** the user explicitly chose ended work shifts with approved-leave
exceptions rather than labeling every calendar day without a punch.
**How to apply:** absence is a read-only monthly presentation, not permission
to create attendance/absence records or introduce wage calculations.

Monthly history displays each day's full date above its own attendance
actions/times or absence/leave badge, on both phone and desktop. Punches may
wrap underneath the date rather than forcing a date-and-events table row.

**Why:** the user requested a date heading followed below by “غائب” or the
day's attendance/break actions, with Thursday 1 October 2026 as the example.
**How to apply:** this is presentation only; preserve the existing accounting
day grouping, original timestamps, approved leave exceptions and absence rules.

Monthly history days are ordered newest first; this applies equally to punch,
absence and leave days. Keep each day's attendance actions in their existing
chronological order.

**Why:** the user explicitly requested the newest date at the top.
**How to apply:** reverse the day ordering only, using the accounting date;
do not reverse individual punches or reassign overnight events.

The user approved explicit leave start/end dates and admin completion of
legacy requests. Never infer a leave period from free text or creation/approval
timestamps. If an approved leave has no reliable period, show a clear notice
and suppress derived absence rather than incorrectly labeling days absent.
Date-only completion must preserve the original approval decision and reply.

**Why:** historical requests lacked structured dates, so their actual periods
cannot be safely reconstructed.
**How to apply:** require valid complete ranges for new leave and approvals.
Only admins may correct old periods. Keep real punch times and the existing
attendance calculation independent of these display changes.

في تبويب سجل التدقيق بالموارد البشرية، اعرض جميع أيام الشهر لكل موظف
حتى دون حركات حضور. الإجازة هنا تعني الإجازة المعتمدة فقط، وليست عطلة
أسبوعية مفترضة. حافظ على الورديات المتعددة والحركات الحقيقية إن وُجدت.

**Why:** طلب المستخدم عرض جميع أيام الشهر والأيام بلا سجلات، ثم اختار
صراحة «الإجازات المعتمدة فقط» عند الاستفسار عن العطلة الأسبوعية.

**How to apply:** الأيام الخالية عرض تقويمي وليست دليل غياب أو إذنًا
لإنشاء حضور تلقائي. لا تملأ ساعاتها بصفر مؤكد، ولا تستنتج إجازة من
طلب معتمد لا توجد له فترة موثوقة.
